# Evidence and Verification Policy

## Golden rule

No accessibility fact may be invented.

If evidence is unavailable: `UNKNOWN`.

## Draft source hierarchy

1.  Government/regulatory source
2.  Professional accessibility audit
3.  Official venue/institution source
4.  Direct on-site measurement/photo with provenance
5.  Trusted institutional dataset
6.  Community observation
7.  Public review
8.  AI inference

Lower-ranked evidence is not automatically false; it requires
appropriate labeling.

## Example

Review: "Ramp near the parking entrance."

Initial structured claim: - attribute: ramp - value: YES - location:
parking entrance - status: REPORTED - source: public review

It must not automatically become VERIFIED.

## Conflicts

Preserve conflicting observations. Do not hide them. Record dates and
mark the current state as CONFLICTING or OUTDATED when appropriate.

## AI/CV

AI may extract candidate facts, identify possible ramps/steps/signage,
classify evidence, and detect contradictions. AI output must remain
unverified until the defined verification process is satisfied.
